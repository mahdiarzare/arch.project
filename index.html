About

Alireza
izadi

Alireza izadi is a mashhad-based architect with over five years of practice in residential, cultural, and public space design. His work is guided by an enduring curiosity about how materials hold silence and how buildings might age with dignity.

He studied architecture at the University of eghbal and completed a postgraduate fellowship at the Architectural Association in London. Since returning to Iran in 2010, he has led projects across ten cities, consistently prioritizing craft over spectacle.

His practice operates with a small team, keeping every decision close to the hand and eye of its makers.
